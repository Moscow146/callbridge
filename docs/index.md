# CallBridge

Take phone calls on your Windows PC: pick any mic and headphones, mute, and more. Android app plus a Windows `.exe`.

The phone stays in your pocket. Its cellular call audio is streamed to the PC, so you hear the caller in the headphones you choose and talk into the microphone you choose. The phone itself stays silent during the call.

## Download

Get `CallBridge.exe` and `callbridge-magisk.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Moscow146/callbridge/releases/latest). Flash the zip in the Magisk app and reboot, start `CallBridge.exe`, scan the QR code from the phone.

## Requirements

- Android 12+ with root (Magisk). Tested on Redmi K50 Ultra, HyperOS 3.
- Windows 10 or 11.
